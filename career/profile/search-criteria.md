# Search criteria

The agent screens every role against this list before tailoring anything. Each role gets **Go**, **Flag** (apply, but tell Essia why), or **Skip** (don't prepare it, and say why).

## Skip: hard filters
- Dutch (or German) language **required**. "Nice to have" is a Flag, not a Skip.
- The posting needs a work/residence status Essia doesn't have, or says no sponsorship. *(The exact rule lives in the private repo's `form-answers.md`, not in this public file.)*
- Asks for **more than 5 years** of experience.
- Pure quota-carrying new-business AE role (cold outbound, full-cycle closing) with no account-management angle. Flag instead if it's hybrid.

## Go: target
- **Tracks, in priority order:** 1. Product (PM / APM / Product Owner / Category & Product / GTM or Product Marketing / Product Ops) · 2. Marketing, Brand, Campaign Manager · 3. Account / Key Account Manager
- **Locations:** Amsterdam and the Randstad (Rotterdam, Utrecht, The Hague, Hilversum, Amstelveen), hybrid or on-site; Netherlands-remote; Dubai/UAE **[CONFIRM: still open to relocating?]**
- **Seniority:** mid-level (2–5 yrs asked). "Senior" is fine when the years required are ≤5.
- **Bonus fit:** design-led, physical + digital products, B2B, sustainability, food and plant-based, consumer brands with a product/configurator angle, SaaS.

## Flag
- Salary listed below **[CONFIRM: your floor]**
- Contract/freelance rather than permanent
- Mostly execution-level ("Specialist", "Coordinator") when a manager-level role exists

## Fit score (shown in every report)
`0–100` = must-have overlap (50) + track match (20) + location (10) + seniority (10) + bonus fit (10). Below 55 → Flag as a stretch.
